Typing Fighter

How to play

Both sides start at 5 HP. Type an unused spell and press Enter to cast it. You go first, then the computer replies if it still has HP. Each side can use each spell once per duel.

You win if the computer reaches 0 HP and lose if you reach 0 HP. If both sides run out of spells, higher HP wins. Equal HP is a draw. Click Restart to play again.

Spells

  • fire deals 1 damage now and 1 Burn damage next round. Wet stops both.
  • air deals 1 damage. If Burn also lands that round, the target takes 2 total.
  • electric deals 1 damage, or 2 if the target is Wet.
  • water deals 1 damage and breaks a Shield or makes an unshielded target Wet for the next round. If it puts out a Burn, neither Water nor Burn deals damage.
  • rock deals no damage. It blocks damage for the rest of this round and the next, unless Water breaks the Shield.
  • cure gives you 1 HP.
